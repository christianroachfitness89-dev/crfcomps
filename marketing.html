Bulk Upload Birthday Leads

Required columns: first_name, last_name, mobile. Optional: email, opt_in (yes/no/true/false), and birthday / birthday_on / dob. Dates can be in any common format (e.g. 15/01/1990 or 1990-01-15) or an Excel date serial number (e.g. 33102) — we convert them automatically. We also auto-calculate the {age} placeholder. Choose the birth month from the dropdown above and it will be applied to every lead as the {birth_month} placeholder. Any other headers become SMS placeholders.
